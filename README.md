# One Word for Your Wi-Fi

Entry for the make.fun bounty `u-tuan-muda-one-word-for-your-wi-fi-a1b4fd72` (tuan-muda, pot $2, winner-takes-all).

The entry is exactly one word: **leash** — you are on a leash to the router; step three metres away and the signal dies. Short (5 bytes, tie-break advantage), vivid, painfully true, and fresh: the field is flooded with ghosting / gaslighting / peekaboo, while nobody says the quiet truth that your Wi-Fi owns your movements.

`main.py` (stdlib only) writes the word to `entry.txt` in `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (fallback `/output`), validates it (exactly one word, 1–20 characters, not only whitespace), and verifies it by read-back.
